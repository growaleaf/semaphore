🚩 Semaphore

1805. You keep a hilltop relay station in a chain of signal towers. Read what arrives. Send it on. Find out what the coast made of it.

This uses the real flag semaphore alphabet — the one signallers actually used. By day fourteen you may be able to read it off a real flag.

How this goes

Two arms, eight positions each. That's the whole alphabet.

  • A message arrives letter by letter through your telescope. Tap the letter you believe you saw before your time runs out.
  • Fog and failing light blur the arms. If you misread a letter, you won't know it — you'll simply send the wrong one.
  • Once you've read the whole message, you set your own arms and send it onward, under the same clock.
  • Your message then passes through other stations on its way to the coast. They have their own eyes, and their own fog.
  • Nothing here fails you outright. A garbled message just keeps going — and the dispatch reads back whatever the chain, including you, made of it.
  • Fourteen days, worsening weather, one crisis night.
